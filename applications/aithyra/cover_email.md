Dear AITHYRA Search Committee,

Please find attached my application for the AI/ML Starting Principal Investigator position at AITHYRA.

I build machine-learning methods for tissue biology (postdoc, Stegle lab, DKFZ/EMBL Heidelberg), working on causal representation learning for cell–cell communication. I built the reference benchmark and widely adopted software for communication inference (Nature Communications 2022; LIANA+, Nature Cell Biology 2024; >300,000 downloads), am co-first (listed first) and co-corresponding author of the Nature Reviews Genetics Perspective charting the field's shift from descriptive to causal models, and now lead the development of the methods that instantiate it — counterfactual inference on tissue graphs (Cellina) and grounded decomposition of context-dependent perturbation effects (KIARA). What I would add to AITHYRA's model-building strength is rigorous evaluation and community-adopted infrastructure, exercised at field scale twice. My research statement details the programme: context-conditioned causal benchmarks, a generalist causal model of communication with mechanistically parameterised interventions, and a closed experimental loop aligned with the institute's lab-automation ambitions.
