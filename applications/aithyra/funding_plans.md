## Funding plan (first five years)

**ERC Starting Grant — the centrepiece.** PhD awarded June 2024; eligible for calls **2027–2031** (2–7 years post-PhD as of 1 January of the call year). I am targeting the **2028 call with AITHYRA as host institution** — Strand 2 (the generalist causal model) and the closed loop of Strand 3 form the StG core, with preliminary results (Cellina, KIARA) maturing on exactly that timeline. Eligibility through 2031 leaves three further attempts inside the window; the programme does not stand or fall on one submission.

**FWF ASTRA Award** (PhD 2–9 years prior; eligible to 2033; €0.5–1M over five years). Earmarked for what the ERC core deliberately excludes: the **community benchmark platform of Strand 1** — its datasets, maintenance, and community process — a self-contained work package submitted early (year 1–2) as the first independent-funding milestone. This partition keeps the two flagship applications non-overlapping: ASTRA builds and sustains the evaluation layer; the ERC builds the models and the loop that consume it.

**FWF Principal Investigator Projects and WWTF Life Sciences calls** — rolling project-level funding for individual strands and students as they mature, matched to Vienna's local landscape as topics align.

**Experimental validation (Strand 3) is resourced explicitly:** from the institute package, a wet-lab-embedded postdoc (hosted with the partner group), a shared technician, and consumables/platform time (planned at ~€100–150k/year — sized for several plate-based gain-of-function dosing rounds per year, with spatial anchoring reserved for gate-clearing hits); scaled through a **Horizon Europe Health-cluster or WWTF consortium with an experimental co-applicant** as the strand matures. Minimum viable wet-lab deliverable on core funds in years 1–2: one benchmarked dose–response round on the lead phenotype. Planned steady-state group: ~4 computational scientists, the wet-lab-embedded postdoc, a shared technician, and dedicated research-software-engineering time.

**Compute.** Current training runs on single-node multi-GPU; the scaling trigger is Strand-2 joint training across atlases, at which point national/EU compute allocations complement the institute package.
