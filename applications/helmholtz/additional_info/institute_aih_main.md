The Institute of AI for Health (AIH) develops solutions for digital health challenges. As an emerging European hub for applied artificial intelligence (AI) and machine learning (ML), we model disease kinetics, develop new drugs and treatments, analyze biomedical images, and integrate multi-omics data for precision medicine. Join us to drive biomedical discovery and build solutions that transform the future of medicine.

The Institute of AI for Health acknowledges generous funding from the High-Tech Agenda Bayern.

## Clinical AI Consultants

Medical AI research is growing at an unprecedented pace, with new ideas and algorithms emerging every day. Yet, only a small fraction of these innovations ever reaches real clinical environments, and even fewer prove their value in prospective trials. Our mission is to bridge the gap between cutting-edge AI research and everyday clinical practice. We help transform ideas into solutions that can truly make a difference in patient care.
