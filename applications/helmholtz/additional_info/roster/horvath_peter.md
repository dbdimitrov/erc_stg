## Research Focus

Our group is dedicated to finding computational solutions to biological problems with an emphasis on understanding processes at a single cell level. Our research focuses on the intersection of biology, engineering and computer science, and combines wet-lab and light microscopy with image analysis and machine learning methods. Our technologies are used to reveal the molecular processes of single cells and to propose personalized therapies for cancers and other diseases.

# BIOMAG

Biological Image Analysis and Machine Learning Group

Recent technological advancements in systems biology, lab automation, and high-throughput microscopy have opened the door to systematic discovery of complex biological systems using high-throughput light microscopy. Modern equipment produce massive amounts of data which cannot be analyzed manually. Automating the analysis process poses several challenges related to computational cell biology. Our group is dedicated to finding computational solutions to biological problems. Our research focuses on the intersection of biology and computer science, and combines wet-lab and light microscopy with image analysis and machine learning methods.

# Hungarian Government Appoints Péter Horváth as State Secretary for Science Policy and Innovation

<span class="c-news-detail__categories"> Computational Health AIH </span> June 4, 2026

The newly formed Hungarian government has appointed Dr. Péter Horváth as State Secretary for Science Policy and Innovation. The internationally recognized computer scientist and AI researcher combines scientific excellence with extensive experience at the intersection of research, technology, and innovation policy. He is Director of the Institute of Biochemistry at the HUN-REN Biological Research Centre in Szeged, Hungary, and also serves as Principal Investigator (PI) at the Institute for AI for Health (AIH) within the Computational Health Center at Helmholtz Munich.

<span lang="EN-US" dir="ltr">Horváth works on computational methods for analyzing biological processes at the single-cell level. His research integrates biology, microscopy, and AI-based image analysis to better understand molecular mechanisms in cells and to support the development of personalized therapies for diseases such as cancer.</span>

<span lang="EN-US" dir="ltr">“We warmly congratulate Péter Horváth on his appointment as State Secretary for Science Policy and Innovation in the newly formed Hungarian government. We are proud to see a highly respected colleague from Helmholtz Munich taking on such an important responsibility for the future of science and innovation in Hungary. His scientific expertise and international experience reflect the strong connection between research and innovation-driven application. We wish him every success in his new role and a steady hand in further strengthening Hungary’s science and innovation landscape,” says Prof. Martin Hrabě de Angelis, member and acting spokesperson of the Executive Board at Helmholtz Munich.</span>

<span lang="EN-US" dir="ltr">In his new role, Péter Horváth is expected to focus on strengthening and further developing Hungary’s research and innovation ecosystem. Key priorities include establishing long-term, predictable research funding, improving career pathways for scientists, and fostering closer collaboration between academia, start-ups, industry, and technology companies.</span>

<span lang="EN-US" dir="ltr">In his initial statement following the appointment, Horváth emphasized the need to address current challenges in the research sector swiftly. Scientific excellence, transparency, fairness, and international competitiveness are set to be the guiding principles of his work. </span>
