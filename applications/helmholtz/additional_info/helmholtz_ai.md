## [Helmholtz AI Conference 2027](https://www.helmholtz.ai/detail/haicon26-ai-for-science-brings-together-600-participants-in-munich/)

### AI for Science

Thank you to everyone who joined us for the Helmholtz AI Conference 2026 (HAICON26), our annual event for AI in science and research across the Helmholtz Association and beyond. From June 8–11, 2026, in Munich, we welcomed over 600 participants from 21 countries to explore the latest advancements in AI and their transformative potential across a wide range of scientific fields. Together, we dove into the possibilities for accelerating discovery and tackling complex challenges with AI, and the conversation is far from over. We're thrilled to announce that HAICON27 will take place in June 2027 in Dresden, Germany. Save the date and join us again as we continue shaping the future of AI in science! Registration details will follow soon.
