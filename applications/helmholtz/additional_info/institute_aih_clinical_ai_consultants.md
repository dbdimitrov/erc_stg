### Who we are

<span lang="EN-US" dir="ltr">As an interdisciplinary team combining expertise in medicine, artificial intelligence, and ethics from both academia and industry, our work is supported by a strong scientific network within the Helmholtz community and beyond. We collaborate with the </span><a href="https://www.helmholtz.ai/applied-ai/ai-consultancy-teams/piraud-team/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer"><u>Helmholtz AI Consultant Team</u></a><span lang="EN-US" dir="ltr">, which brings more than seven years of experience in scientific AI consulting in health research.</span>

## What we offer

We support medical professionals throughout the entire lifecycle of AI research projects—from early ideation to execution and real-world clinical deployment. By combining consulting experience with strong research networks, we help transform promising concepts into prospective trials designed to evaluate AI under real clinical conditions. Our approach is practical, collaborative, and focused on achieving sustained clinical impact.

## How we work

### From Bedside to Bytes – and back

<span lang="EN-US" dir="ltr">Great clinical innovations often start at the bedside. Clinicians experience everyday challenges firsthand and frequently have clear ideas about how processes, diagnostics, or treatments could be improved. However, turning these ideas into functional AI solutions requires time, technical expertise, and structured development. Our goal is to work alongside clinicians and transform real clinical needs into practical AI applications which hold their promises at the bedside.</span>

### Bedside ideation

![]([data-uri-image-removed])

<span id="c132295"></span>

<span lang="EN-US" dir="ltr">As a clinician, you are closest to real-world problems and understand where current systems fall short. Many healthcare professionals already have concrete ideas for improvement, but lack the time or technical resources to explore them further. Even the development of a simple prototype requires extensive expertise in data science, software development, and algorithmic validation. We believe that the best ideas start with clinicians, and we are here to help turn them into reality.</span>

## Realization
