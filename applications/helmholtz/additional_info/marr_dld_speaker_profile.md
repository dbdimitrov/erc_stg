## <span id="span-73-3311811" class="ct-span">Helmholtz Munich</span>

<span id="span-78-3311811" class="ct-span oxy-stock-content-styles"> </span>

Carsten Marr is the Founding Director of the <a href="https://www.helmholtz-munich.de/en/aih" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Institute of AI for Health at Helmholtz Munich</a>, a European center for applied artificial intelligence. He founded his research group at Helmholtz Munich in 2013 and became Deputy Director of the <a href="https://www.helmholtz-munich.de/en/icb" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Institute of Computational Biology</a>. In projects with life scientists and physicians, Marr was one of the first to apply deep learning to biomedical data.
