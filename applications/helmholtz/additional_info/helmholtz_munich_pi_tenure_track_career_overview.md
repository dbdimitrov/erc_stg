## FAQ

### How do I apply to Helmholtz Munich?

![]([data-uri-image-removed])

<span id="c79119"></span>

You can submit your application via the online application form of the respective <a href="https://www.helmholtz-munich.de/en/career/jobs" target="_blank">job advertisement</a>.  
If you have not found what you are looking for in our job openings, please subscribe to our Job Alerts, so you won't miss any suitable positions: <a href="/en/career/jobs-filter#c84948" target="_blank">Job Newsletter</a>.

### Can I send the application by post or e-mail?

![]([data-uri-image-removed])

<span id="c79120"></span>

Please send your application exclusively via our online questionnaire. This will enable us to process your application more quickly and in compliance with data protection regulations, so we can give you earlier feedback.

### Can I do my doctorate/PhD at Helmholtz Munich?

![]([data-uri-image-removed])

<span id="c79121"></span>

Doctorates are possible at our research center in cooperation with universities. You can find out more on our doctoral program at our <a href="https://www.helmholtz-munich.de/en/helena" target="_blank">Helmholtz Munich Graduate School</a> and the [scientific career development](/en/career/scientific-talent-and-career-development) pages. 

### What does TVöD mean?

![]([data-uri-image-removed])

<span id="c79122"></span>

TVöD refers to the collective agreement for the public sector. It regulates payment, holiday entitlement but also pension benefits etc.. More detailed information such as the current collective agreement TVöD Bund and Federal Pay Scale can be found e.g. at <a href="https://oeffentlicher-dienst.info/c/t/rechner/tvoed/bund?id=tvoed-bund-2026&amp;matrix=1" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer">https://oeffentlicher-dienst.info/c/t/rechner/tvoed/bund?id=tvoed-bund-2026&amp;matrix=1</a> (Information only available in German).

### How do I reach the main campus in Neuherberg or any other of your locations?

![]([data-uri-image-removed])

<span id="c79123"></span>

<a href="https://www.helmholtz-munich.de/en/address/our-locations" target="_blank">Here</a> you can find the address of our campus in Neuherberg and all our other locations in Munich and Germany.

## Application Process

Submit application

Send your application via our online application tool -\> Please find the link below each job advertisement.

Confirmation

You will receive an email confirmation when you have successfully submitted your application.

Interviews

If your profile fits, we will invite you to an interview via telephone, video conference or in person. There might be further interviews depending on the position you applied for.

Contract offer

The succesful candidate will receive a verbal contract offer.

Works Council Approval

Our Works Council will review and assess your future contract, as soon as it is approved the process continues.

Employment Contract

As a final step, you will receive your original employment contract for signature.
