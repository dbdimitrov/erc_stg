## PASTE BLOCK (≈2,400 characters)

**Basic qualifications**

- **Degrees.** BSc (Hons) Cell Biology, Stirling (First Class); MSc Bioinformatics, Glasgow
  (Distinction); PhD in Computational Biology, Heidelberg University (2024).

- **Large-scale genetic and multi-omics data integrated with clinical/phenotypic data.**
  My cell–cell communication analysis in the Human Cell Atlas study of inflammatory gut disease
  (*Nature*, 2024) pinpointed inflammatory-niche signalling axes in patient tissue. I ran the
  differential-expression and proteomics enrichment analyses for a CKD pharmacology study
  (*Scientific Reports*, 2025), and worked on transcriptomics/proteomics integration across CKD
  patient cohorts with clinical collaborators in Ghent. My PhD was funded by the MSCA network
  STRATEGY-CKD, embedding me in a clinical nephrology consortium throughout. I additionally
  **conceived and direct** EHRx, a project interpreting transformer foundation models of electronic
  health records — steering internal features to expose encoded comorbidity patterns and testing
  them for polygenic-risk enrichment against UK Biobank genetic and clinical data.

- **Python and production-quality analysis.** I am developer and maintainer of LIANA and LIANA+
  (`liana-py`, 300,000+ installs) and a co-developer of `decoupleR` (~470,000 installs) — shipped
  with version control, continuous integration, unit testing and documentation, not just published.

- **End-to-end analytic pipelines in an R&D setting.** LIANA+ (*Nature Cell Biology*, 2024) is an
  end-to-end framework — ingestion, QC, analysis, reporting — applied across single-cell, spatial
  and multi-modal datasets. Before my PhD I built terabyte-scale ETL pipelines as a data engineer
  at Experian.

- **Communication to non-specialists.** Corresponding author on a *Nature Reviews Genetics*
  Perspective and co-author on reviews in *Nature Immunology* and the Single-cell Best Practices
  guidance; invited talks at VIB Ghent (2026), the GAF summer school in Sofia (2025) and the
  MOPITAS symposium at SDU (2024); co-organised an ECCB tutorial and the scverse Hackathon.

- **Multidisciplinary teams and competing priorities.** I work across the Stegle and
  Saez-Rodríguez groups and three consortia while directing five concurrent projects.

- **ML / advanced statistics on molecular and clinical data** — graph and variational deep learning
  (Cellina, senior and co-corresponding author), foundation-model interpretability (EHRx),
  ensemble enrichment methods (`decoupleR`).
- **Publications, presentations, external collaborations** — 4,000+ citations, h-index 14;

- **Scalable compute and governed data** — containerised, reproducible analysis on multi-million-cell
