## Unveiling the role of sex in the metabolism of indoxyl sulfate and apixaban

*Pina-Beltran et al., Scientific Reports (2025) · **Author role: Contributing author (2nd)***

**Problem:** Chronic kidney disease (CKD) patients face coexisting thrombosis and bleeding risk, and show increased bleeding under the direct factor Xa inhibitor apixaban versus non-CKD patients. Whether sex modifies the metabolism of apixaban and the uremic toxin indoxyl sulfate (IS) — and thereby bleeding risk — was untested, despite females being underrepresented in trials (e.g., DAPA-CKD 33%, ARISTOTLE 35%).

**Approach:** In vivo intoxication model in C57BL/6J wild-type and Ahr−/− mice of both sexes (6 groups/sex: ±IS in drinking water, ±apixaban by gavage, ±AhR knockout). Plasma IS quantified by HPLC and apixaban by LC-MS; tail-bleeding time measured. mRNA of efflux transporters and cytochromes (Abcb1a/b, Abcg2, Cyp1a1/1a2/3a11/1b1/2e1, Sult1a1, Slc22a6/OAT1, Slc22a8/OAT3) profiled by RT-qPCR in liver, ileum, kidney. Whole-liver proteomics (60 samples) by FASP + timsTOF Pro DIA, processed with DIA-NN (library-free).

**Key contributions:** IS and apixaban accumulate more in females, with higher bleeding times; sex — not treatment or genotype — is the dominant driver of expression variance. Females show higher hepatic Sult1a1 (favoring IS production) but lower Abcg2 and Cyp3a11 (reduced apixaban efflux/metabolism); kidney OAT1/OAT3 are sex-dimorphic. Proteomics confirmed mRNA trends and revealed sex-dependent regulation of coagulation, bile acid, xenobiotic, and fatty acid metabolism pathways.

**Data & tools:** DIA-NN v1.8 (https://github.com/vdemichev/DiaNN); OmniPath (UniProt-to-gene mapping); VSN normalization; LIMMA moderated t-tests; decoupleR univariate linear models (ulm) with MSigDB hallmark (mouse) for pathway enrichment; GraphPad Prism. Proteomics against UniProtKB mouse + cRAP.

**Relevance to future work:** Exemplifies Daniel's likely contribution — the proteomics differential-expression and decoupleR/MSigDB functional-enrichment analysis — applied to a pharmacology/toxicology model, showing his footprint-analysis toolkit (decoupleR, OmniPath) generalizes beyond single-cell to bulk proteomics and sex-stratified preclinical study design.
