## Clarifying the murk: unveiling bacterial dynamics in response to crude oil pollution, Corexit-dispersant, and natural sunlight in the Gulf of Mexico

*Matallana-Surget et al., Frontiers in Marine Science (2024) · **Author role: Contributing author (middle)***

**Problem:** The 2010 Deepwater Horizon spill triggered massive use of the dispersant Corexit EC9500A, yet the combined effects of crude oil, dispersant, and natural sunlight on the composition and functional activity of Gulf of Mexico (GoM) marine bacterial communities were unexplored, especially at the protein level.

**Approach:** Comparative metaproteomics across 8 mesocosm conditions (70 L Tedlar bags): seawater control, oil-only, Corexit-only, and combined oil+Corexit, each under sunlight (UV+PAR) and dark, incubated 24 h in GoM surface waters. 16S rRNA amplicon sequencing (515F/926R, Illumina MiSeq, QIIME v1.9.1, UPARSE OTUs) built per-condition protein search databases. Gel-free shotgun proteomics (trypsin digest, Eksigent NanoLC 400 + AB SCIEX TripleTOF 5600, DDA) identified active proteins; taxonomy/function annotated linking who-does-what.

**Key contributions:** Oil and especially Corexit sharply reduced taxonomic diversity within 24 h, selecting for hydrocarbon degraders; sunlight amplified dispersant toxicity. Alteromonas dominated oil+Corexit metaproteomes (transporters, motility, carbon/amino-acid metabolism, cellular defense). Functional shifts were driven by oxidative stress, protein turnover, DNA repair, and cell division. Glyoxylate-bypass enzymes enriched in contaminant treatments mapped to Rhodobacterales/Alteromonadales. Marinomonas was active only in the dark. The study provides in situ bioremediation proxies and predictions of biogeochemical-cycle impacts.

**Data & tools:** mPies v0.9 metaproteomics annotation (https://github.com/johanneswerner/mPies) — Diamond alignment vs NCBI-nr and UniProt SwissProt, LCA via MEGAN (bit score >80); ProteinPilot 5.0.1 (Paragon), 1% protein-level FDR. Raw data at iProx PXD048022.

**Relevance to future work:** Daniel's middle-author contribution reflects bioinformatic/data-analysis work (metaproteomic annotation, taxonomy-function linkage, multivariate/PCA analysis) during his University of Stirling affiliation — evidence of environmental-microbiome and metaproteomics experience predating his computational-biomedicine single-cell work.
