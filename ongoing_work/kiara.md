# KIARA: Learning how context modulates perturbation effects in single-cell and spatial omics

*Planned submission: Nature Methods (AI-focused methods contribution) · target: bioRxiv preprint concurrent with journal submission, Q1 2027 · **Author role: first + corresponding — the candidate's hands-on AI flagship** · Status: work in progress; core model and benchmark suite implemented, applications preliminary.*

**Problem:** The perturbation-modeling field (CPA, GEARS, scGPT-class models) concentrates on *predicting* responses in unseen contexts, treating context as a nuisance dimension to generalize across. The biological question is frequently the inverse: *how does context — cell type, tissue niche, and ultimately time — reshape what a perturbation does?* No existing method delivers that modulation as an explicit, quantified, interpretable object, and none does so uniformly across dissociated and spatial data. Worse, in jointly trained models the question is not even well-posed: a flexible model can freely shuffle variance between baseline, main effect, and interaction, so "the context effect" is whatever the optimizer happened to park there.

**The idea — attribution by construction:** KIARA makes the decomposition itself the deliverable, and makes it trustworthy by *grounding every term*. The control baseline is anchored to reproduce observed control cells essentially exactly; the pooled perturbation effect is fit before any interaction exists; each context term is then attached with the rest of the model frozen, initialized at exactly zero effect, and shrunk back toward zero — so any nonzero interaction or niche term is, by construction, signal the frozen stages demonstrably could not explain. Joint models leave attribution to the optimizer; KIARA removes the ambiguity mechanically. The interaction term becomes a finding, not a fudge factor. This staged partialling-out is double-ML-inspired (Frisch–Waugh–Lovell in spirit: nuisance components fit and frozen, the target term estimated on residual signal) — stated precisely, without claiming DML's semiparametric guarantees; cross-fitting is a candidate rigor upgrade for the paper.

## Model

One additive decomposition spans both data regimes (written in log1p expression space; under the negative-binomial likelihood the residual terms compose in count space via standardized residuals):

$$
\mu \;=\; \underbrace{b_c}_{\text{cell-type baseline}} \;+\; \underbrace{e\,W_e^{\top}}_{\text{pooled perturbation effect}} \;+\; \underbrace{z_{\mathrm{int}}\,(W_e + \Delta_c)^{\top}}_{\text{cell-type} \times \text{perturbation interaction}} \;+\; \underbrace{s\,(W_e + \Delta_{\mathrm{niche}})^{\top}}_{\text{spatial niche effect}}
$$

**Stage 1 — the anchored base.** Each perturbation is embedded as a programme-space effect $e = \mathrm{pert} \cdot g$, with a variational Normal posterior on $g$ whose KL is reweighted by inverse per-perturbation cell counts (rare perturbations are not drowned out) and can optionally be seeded from prior knowledge. The programme-to-gene loadings $W_e$ carry a variational **regularized (Finnish) horseshoe** — structured global × per-programme × local scales with a slab capping the tails — so programmes stay sparse and nameable; an optional prior-knowledge mask additionally gates warm-up and shapes the prior scale (prior knowledge is an inductive bias throughout, never a requirement). The per-(cell-type, gene) baseline $b_c$ is a free term that, after base training, is **reference-anchored**: the mean control-cell residual is folded back into $b_c$ per cell type, driving the control residual to ~0 by construction. Everything downstream is measured against a baseline calibrated to the observed control state.

**Stage 2 — the interaction tensor (frozen base).** With the base frozen, the cell-type × perturbation interaction enters through a gate

$$
z_{\mathrm{int}} \;=\; m_p \;+\; d[p, c], \qquad d \in \mathbb{R}^{P \times C \times S},
$$

a shared per-perturbation programme activation $m_p$ plus a per-(perturbation, cell-type) deviation tensor $d$. $d$ is zero-initialized, so the gate *starts as the pooled model* and cell-type-specific deviations open only as the data insists; the shared $m_p$ stays unpenalized, so shrinking $d$ falls back to the pooled response rather than eroding it. $d$ is the ct×pert interaction proper — the object read out — and carries selectable structured sparsity: a **multi-level regularized horseshoe** (global × per-perturbation × per-(pert, ct) budget × per-programme scales, with a slab and a half-normal on the global log-scale that fixes the MAP degeneracy) or an **exact top-k** budget with straight-through gradients, making "how many programmes does cell type $c$ reshape under perturbation $p$" a parameter one literally reads off. Built-in diagnostics report per-combination active-programme counts, so a genuine cell-type-specific finding is cleanly distinguishable from the pooled response. The gate's activation is decoded through a per-cell-type decoder **anchored at the frozen base loadings**: initialized at $W_e$ and penalized back toward it, so $\Delta_c$ is an interpretable per-cell-type *rotation* of the shared gene programmes, not a fresh decoder.

**Stage 2′ — the spatial niche (frozen base).** For spatial data, a **GATv2 graph-attention encoder** embeds each cell's neighborhood — input: the cell-type one-hot concatenated with the perturbation effect $e$ — into a **cell-level niche latent $s$** in the same programme space. $s$ is trained with a **label-free neighborhood contrastive (InfoNCE) objective**: positives are a cell's spatial-graph neighbors, negatives the other in-batch cells, no domain or cell-type labels — plus a KL to a standard-normal prior and the same frozen-base residual loss. The niche decoder is always anchored at $W_e$, so niche effects are expressed in the *same coordinate system* as perturbation effects — the niche is treated as a spatially derived perturbation, and programmes learned from dissociated screens transfer as the reference frame for tissue. The per-edge GATv2 attention weights are extractable, adding a "which neighbors drive this niche effect" readout on top of "which programmes."

**Identified by design.** The claim is estimation-level and mechanical, not theorem-level: because the baseline is pinned to controls, the pooled effect is fit before the interaction exists, and every residual term starts at zero effect and is shrunk toward it, the decomposition's attribution is unambiguous by construction. Every learned object along the way is directly interpretable — $g$ (perturbation → programmes), $W_e$ (programmes → genes), $d$ (cell-type-specific programme usage), $\Delta_c$ (cell-type programme rotations), attention (neighbor influence). Sequential residual learning is also the generality mechanism: cell type, niche, and (planned) time are all "just contexts" attached the same way.

**Follows up on Cellina:** KIARA is the direct successor to the lab lineage Cellina opened (Moeed et al., supervised student work). Cellina established supervised disentanglement of intrinsic identity from extrinsic microenvironment on tissue graphs, enabling counterfactual queries at the *representation* level ("what would this cell do in a different niche?"). KIARA takes the next step: it makes the context effects themselves explicit at the *effect* level — decomposed, sparse, and quantified per (cell type, perturbation, programme) — and extends the intrinsic/extrinsic split into a grounded, staged estimation framework spanning dissociated and spatial regimes.

**Evidence to date (demonstrated in the repo's benchmark suite):** leave-one-group-out cross-donor/sample cross-validation on six datasets spanning both regimes — IFN-β-stimulated PBMCs (Kang), a 20-cytokine screen across donors (Parse), 140 compounds (OP3), TTN cardiomyopathy under nested CV (Reichart), CRC spatial, and a control-free liver spatial dataset scored by reconstruction. Interaction recovery outperforms additive and interaction linear models and mean-shift baselines; disentanglement outperforms scVI and ContrastiveVI; learned programmes are seed-stable (Jaccard / Precision@k stability across seeds). **Planned before submission:** head-to-head arms against the perturbation-prediction class (CPA, biolord, MrVI, SAMS-VAE) repurposed for effect decomposition — comparisons framed on KIARA's terms (decomposition quality, not just predictive fit).

**Planned applications (preliminary — directional, not committed):** flagship candidate is an in-house heart-organoid dataset with a time course and perturbations, where time enters as an additional context conditioning the interaction term — a direct test of the "everything is a context" generality claim. Spatial application candidate: CRC spatial data (Crowell) for niche-modulated perturbation effects. Both are early ideas and expected to evolve.

**Relevance to AI angle (group-leader narrative):** KIARA is the evidence that the candidate personally designs and implements modern probabilistic machine learning — structured Bayesian sparsity (multi-level regularized horseshoes), graph attention networks with interpretable attention readouts, label-free contrastive representation learning, and staged, causal-inference-flavored estimation — rather than only supervising or packaging it. It completes a three-part AI portfolio: field-standard infrastructure (LIANA+, OmniPath ecosystem), supervised deep-learning research (Cellina, student first-authored), and now a hands-on methodological flagship (KIARA, first + corresponding). Intellectually it executes the research line of the candidate's own Nature Reviews Genetics Perspective — moving single-cell analysis from prediction toward interpretation and causal understanding of context.

- **Demonstrated now:** the full staged model on dissociated + spatial data; 6-dataset LOGO CV; wins over linear/mean-shift baselines and scVI/ContrastiveVI on disentanglement; seed-stability of programmes; reference anchoring and the ctpert tensor gate validated in cross-validation.
- **Planned (before Q1 2027 submission):** prediction-class baseline arms (CPA, biolord, MrVI, SAMS-VAE); application analyses; possible cross-fitting for the residual stages.
- **Preliminary (may change):** heart-organoid flagship (time as context is *not yet implemented* in the model); CRC (Crowell) spatial application choice.
- **Sparsity trade-off (frame positively):** the structured sparsity priors on $d$ are opt-in regularizers that buy exact per-(cell-type, perturbation) programme budgets at essentially no accuracy cost — at most a minor decrease on one metric in one dataset in the repo's validation — the right trade for an interpretability-focused method.
- **Open risks:** (1) the acronym's "knowledge-driven" framing overcommits relative to the PK-optional positioning — either soften the expansion or foreground PK-optionality early; (2) double-ML language must stay at "inspired, stated precisely," and identifiability language at "identified by design / unambiguous attribution by construction" — no theorem claims without a formal result; (3) benchmark suite currently lacks the comparison class Nature Methods reviewers will demand (see Planned); (4) six-month timeline assumes applications converge — the benchmarks are the mature half.
